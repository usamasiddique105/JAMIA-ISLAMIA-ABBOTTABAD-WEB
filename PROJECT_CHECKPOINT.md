# Permanent Project Checkpoint — Jamia Islamia Abbottabad

**Date Of Checkpoint**: July 24, 2026
**Institution**: Jamia Islamia Abbottabad, Pakistan (Est. 2003)
**Affiliation**: Wifaqul Madaris Al-Arabia Pakistan

---

## 1. Vision & Purpose
To establish a world-class, multi-lingual digital portal for Jamia Islamia Abbottabad that reflects its 23-year legacy as a leading center for Islamic learning, Darul Ifta research, Quranic sciences, and Hadith studies. The portal connects students, scholars, researchers, donors, and the public globally with authentic fatwas, academic results, digital publications, and institutional news.

---

## 2. Comprehensive UI/UX Design Philosophy
- **Aesthetic**: Traditional Islamic institutional design with intricate gold geometric borders, warm cream canvas, deep mahogany wood accents, and refined Nastaleeq typography.
- **Color Palette**:
  - Primary Warm Canvas: `#F8F4EC`
  - Deep Bronze/Mahogany Wood: `#5C4632`
  - Islamic Gold / Accent: `#B88A3B`
  - Dark Mode Slate: `#0F172A`
  - Charcoal Primary Text: `#2B2B2B`
- **Typography Scale**:
  - Main Heading: `36px - 42px` (Desktop)
  - Section Heading: `28px - 32px`
  - Navigation Menu: `18px - 20px` (Medium-bold, `.font-urdu` / `.font-arabic` / `.font-inter`)
  - Buttons: `14px - 16px`
  - Body Text: `16px - 18px` with generous line-height (`leading-[2.0]` for Urdu)
  - Small Labels: `12px - 14px`

---

## 3. Key Completed Sections & Features
1. **Multi-Lingual System**: Full trilingual support (Urdu, Arabic, English) with instant direction handling (`rtl` vs `ltr`) and complete translations.
2. **Top Bar & Ticker**: Live Hijri date calculator, prayer times widget for Abbottabad, global site search modal trigger, dark mode toggle, language selector.
3. **Institutional Header**:
   - Compact institutional seal logo (`JamiaSealLogo`) with gold dashed ring, book emblem, and "EST. 1951 PAKISTAN" medallion.
   - Dual-CTA action buttons: "آن لائن فتویٰ پوچھیں" (Ask Online Fatwa) and "عطیات و زکوٰۃ" (Donations & Zakat).
4. **Header Navigation Menu**: 9 main institutional links with dropdowns for Departments, Exams, Publications, News, and Contact.
5. **Traditional Islamic Hero Slider**: Full-width authentic Jamia Islamia Abbottabad madrasa building photo background (featuring the white minaret, green dome, and arched arcade) overlaid with a soft dark green & warm sepia gradient (25-35% opacity), automatic 6-second fade transition, navigation arrows, and dot indicators featuring:
   - Clean Unobstructed Background View: Removed central floating logo container/checkerboard box completely to display the continuous, full photo of the Jamia Islamia Abbottabad madrasa building (white minaret, green dome, arched arcade, trees, and sky) cleanly behind the hero section text.
   - Urdu Name: `جامعہ اسلامیہ ایبٹ آباد` in prominent Nastaliq.
   - English Name: `JAMIA ISLAMIA ABBOTTABAD, PAKISTAN` in Cinzel typography.
   - Concise Tagline: `مرکزِ علومِ قرآن، حدیث، فقہ، عربی، دعوت و تحقیق`.
   - Triple Action Buttons: "مزید معلومات" (About Us), "دارالافتاء" (Darul Ifta), and "عطیات" (Donations).
6. **Quick Access Portals**: 8 quick access cards below hero (Darul Ifta, Ask Fatwa, Fatwa Archive, Exam Results, Announcements, Media Gallery, Library, Contact).
7. **Online Darul Ifta & Fatwa Archive**: Category filter, search bar, fatwa detail modal with official seal/download/copy/print functionality, and online query submission modal.
8. **Exam Results Portal**: Search by Roll Number, Class (Dars-e-Nizami / Takhassus / Hifz), and Year with verified digital marksheet preview.
9. **Digital Library & Publications**: View monthly journal "Nida-e-Jamia", books catalog, and downloadable PDFs.
10. **Donation & Zakat Portal**: Transparent bank accounts (Meezan Bank, HBL), Easypaisa, JazzCash details, and Zakat/Sadaqah calculation helper.
11. **Responsive Design**: Flawless execution across Mobile (375px+), Tablet (768px+), and Desktop (1280px+).
